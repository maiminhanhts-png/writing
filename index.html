12:15  MYSTERY 2

Why does rain matter?

Rainy seasons and dry seasons: rain does not fall all year. Some areas have two of each.
Too little rain
➜
Crops dry out
➜
Less food
Famine (nạn đói) = many people do not have enough food for a long time.
Very little rain does not always mean famine, but it makes farming difficult.
